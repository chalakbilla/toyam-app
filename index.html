WhatsApp export organizer

Toyam

Organize policies from a WhatsApp chat export and keep the resulting files on your device or send selected files to Google Drive.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Toyam processes chat export files you choose to open. Chat text and extracted policy files are stored locally on your device. Toyam does not operate a service that receives your chat exports.

Sharing and Google Drive

If you choose to share or upload files, the files you select are sent to the destination you choose. For Google Drive uploads, Toyam transmits the selected policy files to Google Drive. Google handles those files under its own terms and privacy policy. You can also use Toyam without choosing a Drive upload.

Information and control

Toyam does not intentionally collect analytics or maintain an account for you. App files and upload activity logs are stored on your device. You can remove saved files and logs using the app or your device's storage controls. Shared or uploaded copies are managed separately by the service or recipient you chose.

Children

Toyam is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Contact

For questions about Toyam or this policy, contact the project maintainer through the Toyam GitHub Issues page.

Toyam is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp, Meta, or Google.
